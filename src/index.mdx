**Mesub** charges a customer's wallet on a schedule, without holding their funds:
the customer approves a plan once, and each period Mesub pulls the price to your
wallet.

Your part is small. You **publish a plan** from the dashboard, add
**`@mesub/node`** to your server with an API key, and ask one question before
serving anything paid: does this customer have access?

**`@mesub/react`** is optional. It adds a subscribe button and a manage window to
your pages, and talks only to your own server.
