<h1 style={{ fontSize: '30px', lineHeight: 1.2, fontWeight: 600, letterSpacing: '-0.02em', textWrap: 'balance' }}>Recurring payments on Solana, billed from your server</h1>

Mesub charges a customer's wallet on a schedule, without holding their funds.
The customer approves a plan once, Mesub pulls the price to your wallet at
every period, and your server asks Mesub who has access.

You publish a plan in the [dashboard](https://mesub.io/dashboard), add
`@mesub/node` to your server, and gate what is paid. A React widget is there
for the wallet part if you want it.

## Before you start

- **A Solana wallet** in a wallet app such as Phantom or Solflare. It signs
  your plans and receives the money.
- **A Mesub account**, at [mesub.io](https://mesub.io/dashboard). To test
  first, on devnet: see [step 0](./docs/quickstart.mdx#step-0-test-money-on-devnet).
- **A server with your own login**, on Node 22 or later. Mesub asks it who is
  signed in, and never sees your users' passwords.
- **React 18 or later**, only for the widget.

## What your customer sees

- **They approve once.** Their wallet signs the plan's terms and the first
  payment. They need no Mesub account.
- **Each payment is capped.** Mesub can take up to the plan's price each period,
  into the plan's receiving wallets, and nothing else. The Solana program
  enforces it, not Mesub's servers.
- **They cancel any time**, on your site or at
  [mesub.io/subscriptions](https://mesub.io/subscriptions) with their wallet.
  Access runs to the end of the period paid.
