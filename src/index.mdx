<h1 style={{ fontSize: '30px', lineHeight: 1.2, fontWeight: 600, letterSpacing: '-0.02em', textWrap: 'balance' }}>Recurring payments on Solana, billed from your server</h1>

Mesub charges a customer's wallet on a schedule, without holding their funds.
The customer approves a plan once, Mesub pulls the price to your wallet at
every period, and your server asks Mesub who has access.

You publish a plan in the [dashboard](https://mesub.io/dashboard), add
`@mesub/node` to your server, and gate what is paid. A React widget is there
for the wallet part if you want it.
