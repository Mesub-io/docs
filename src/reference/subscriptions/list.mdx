<Endpoint id="listSubscriptions">

A customer's subscriptions to your project's plans, newest first: what a "my subscriptions" page needs. A customer with nothing is not an error: `data` is empty.

<More>

Expired checkouts are listed too, and `access` says they grant nothing. A subscription replaced by a newer one of the same wallet (`superseded`) is left out, though its id still answers on [Retrieve](./retrieve.mdx).

</More>

</Endpoint>
