One shape everywhere: [retrieve](./retrieve.mdx) and [list](./list.mdx) answer
it, [submit](./submit.mdx) and every confirm answer it under `subscription`,
and every [webhook](../webhooks/overview.mdx) carries it under `data`.

`access`, `payment_status` and `access_until` are what
[`GET /v1/access`](../access/check.mdx) answers for the same subscription, so
you know whether it grants access without asking again.
[Lifecycle](../../docs/lifecycle.mdx) says what each status means.
