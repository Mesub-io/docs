<Endpoint id="createSubscription">

Reserves a subscription to one of your plans for the wallet named, and builds what that wallet signs: the terms, as a message, then the transaction, without sending it. Hand both to [Submit](./submit.mdx).

Called again for the same plan, wallet and customer while nothing has landed, it answers the same subscription with a fresh transaction. The customer is the `external_id`, else the `email`, else nobody. Another customer naming the same wallet gets a subscription of its own: only the one the wallet signs for lands.

A wallet whose subscription to this plan stopped comes back over its existing authorisation: a new subscription, with `costs.rent` at zero.

On a plan with an end date, `terms.message` says that the last charge is in full and that access stops when the plan ends. When the plan ends before a second charge can be made, it says the charge is a single one.

</Endpoint>
