<Endpoint id="retrieveSubscription">

One subscription: its status, whether it grants access and until when, its wallet, email and `external_id`, the period it last paid for, and what comes next. `access`, `payment_status` and `access_until` are what [Check access](../access/check.mdx) answers for it.

A `superseded` id, left out of the list, still answers here, so an id you stored keeps working.

</Endpoint>
