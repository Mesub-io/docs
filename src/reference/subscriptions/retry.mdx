<Endpoint id="retrySubscription">

Runs the charge of the missed period now, rather than at its next retry: for a customer who topped up their wallet and wants their access back from your page. Nothing to sign: the wallet approved the plan when it subscribed.

The charge runs in the background, with the checks and records of a scheduled one. The answer is the subscription, still `unpaid`: the outcome comes as `subscription.renewed` or `subscription.payment_failed`, and on the subscription.

Refused before anything is queued when it cannot succeed, as the chain reads now, so that it never spends a retry for nothing: the wallet holds less than the period costs, no longer approves the subscription, or its token account is frozen, or the last charge found its authorisation closed.

On Free, a retry by hand is one of the missed period's three, whoever makes it, at least ten minutes apart, and none within two minutes of the end of the missed period. On Dev and Business it runs beside the schedule, and the wait between two doubles.

</Endpoint>
