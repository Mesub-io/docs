<Endpoint id="freshSubscriptionTransaction">

A fresh transaction for a subscription still waiting for its signature, with the terms it names. A transaction lives 150 blocks, about half a minute: `transaction_expires_at` estimates when they run out, `last_valid_block_height` says it. The terms live five minutes. So have the terms signed first, and call this route when the wallet is ready for the transaction: its 150 blocks start here, not while the customer reads. Have the wallet sign it at once, then send it with the terms signature to [Submit](./submit.mdx). Always sign the transaction of the last call.

To know whether a terms signature you hold is still good, compare `terms.message` with the message the wallet signed. The same text is the same terms: submit with the signature you hold. Another text is new terms: have the wallet sign this message, since submit refuses the old signature with `invalid_terms_signature`.

The terms come back unchanged while they have more than two minutes left before `terms.expires_at`. New terms come after that, after a submit that spent them (one answered `transaction_expired` may have), or when the plan changed on chain.

This route reserves nothing and takes no body: the wallet, the plan and the customer are the subscription's own. For a subscription that expired, call [Create](./create.mdx) again.

</Endpoint>
