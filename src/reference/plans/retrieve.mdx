<Endpoint id="retrievePlan">

One plan: its name, its price in the smallest unit and as a person counts it, its period, its token, its status, its end date, and whether it takes new subscribers now.

A plan past its end date takes no new subscriber: `available` is false, and [Create a subscription](../subscriptions/create.mdx) answers `plan_ended`.

</Endpoint>
