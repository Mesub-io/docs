<Endpoint id="closeSubscription">

Builds the transaction that closes the authorisation of a cancelled or stopped subscription whose end has passed, or at once of one stopped with `stop_reason` `authority_closed`. `closable` on the subscription says whether it can close now, and `closable_from` from when. The subscription's own wallet signs and sends it, and gets its rent back. Then you [confirm](./confirm-close.mdx).

</Endpoint>
