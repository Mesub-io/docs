<Endpoint id="cancelSubscription">

Builds the transaction that cancels an `active`, `unpaid` or `stopped` subscription. The subscription's own wallet signs and sends it, then you [confirm](./confirm-cancel.mdx). Nothing changes until the confirmation.

<More>

A subscription that is paid up keeps its access until the end of the period. A late or stopped one has none, and its missed period is never collected.

</More>

</Endpoint>
