<Endpoint id="webhook_subscription_stopped">

Mesub stopped charging after missed payments. `data.stop_reason` says why: `out_of_retries`, `retry_deadline_passed` or `authority_closed`.

<More>

Mesub posts this to every endpoint of the project subscribed to it. Answer with a 2xx within 10 seconds.
