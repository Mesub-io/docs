<Endpoint id="webhook_subscription_access_revoked">

Access ended: the subscription grants no access now, and did just before. It stopped, ended, failed a payment on Free, a cancellation reached its end, its plan ended, or a collection blocked on Mesub's or the merchant's side reached the end of the access it keeps (`access_until`). Sent at the change, or, for a date that passed with nothing else happening, a few seconds after it: `detail.changed_at` is then that date, to the millisecond.

<More>

Drive the customer's access from `subscription.access_granted` and `subscription.access_revoked` alone: they are sent only when access actually changes, and every change sends one. The other events say why. Apply them in the order of `sequence`, since deliveries may arrive in any order: the last one applied says whether the customer has access. `data.access` is false.

Mesub posts this to every endpoint of the project subscribed to it. Answer with a 2xx within 10 seconds.

</More>
