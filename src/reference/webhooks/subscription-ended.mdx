<Endpoint id="webhook_subscription_ended">

It is over. `data.end_reason` says why. A cancellation reaching its end sends no event of its own.

<More>

On a plan with an end date, a subscription still running or late is ended at the plan's end, within minutes of it, whatever period it paid for: this event then carries `end_reason: "plan_ended"`.

Mesub posts this to every endpoint of the project subscribed to it. Answer with a 2xx within 10 seconds.
