<Endpoint id="webhook_subscription_paused">

Not sent yet: Mesub turns this event on later. A seat was parked: the project now holds more subscribers than its tier allows (it moved to a smaller tier, or a subscription confirmed late filled it), and Mesub stopped charging the newest ones rather than any within the limit. Not a pause the subscriber asked for: nothing changed on chain, and the status stays as it was.

<More>

The subscription keeps access to the end of the period it paid for, and no further: `data.paused` is true, `data.next_charge_at` null. Its `subscription.access_revoked` comes when that access ends, or right after this event, with a higher `sequence`, when it had already ended. The project moving back to a tier with room sends `subscription.unpaused`.

Mesub posts this to every endpoint of the project subscribed to it. Answer with a 2xx within 10 seconds.

</More>
