## In short

- An **organization** holds projects, their members and their billing.
- Every Mesub account has a **personal organization**. You can create others
  and invite members.
- The **organization wallet** signs the organization's plans and pays its
  Dev tier. Only the Owner sets it.
- What it has not signed yet waits in **To sign**.
- A project's tier changes in **Billing**.

## Your first sign-in

The dashboard asks three things, one per screen:

1. **Name your organization.**
2. **Create your first project**: one project per app that takes payments.
3. **Connect your AI agent**, through the [MCP server](./mcp.mdx). You can
   skip it.

## Organizations

Your personal organization stays as long as your Mesub account does. To
create another one, open the organization menu, then **Create an
organization**. You become its Owner.

The organization's own pages are **Overview**, **Projects**, **To sign**,
**Activity**, **Members**, **AI & agents**, **Billing** and **Organization
settings**. Each project has **Overview**, **Plans**, **Subscriptions**,
**Payments**, **Developers** and **Settings**.

## Roles

| Role   | What it can do                                                                  |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner  | Everything. Alone: the organization wallet, billing, Admins, deleting a project |
| Admin  | API keys, webhooks, receiving wallets, projects. Invites and removes Members    |
| Member | Sees everything. Prepares plans, handles subscribers, connects AI agents        |

There is one Owner. To change who it is, the Owner uses **Transfer
ownership** and picks an Admin, who becomes the Owner. The old Owner becomes
an Admin. This does not hand over any wallet key, nor control of plans
already on chain.

To add someone, open **Members**, then **Invite members**. The invite is for
that email address only, and expires in 7 days. The person opens the link
and presses **Join**, followed by the organization's name. Removing a member ends their access
at their next click and disconnects their AI agents. Nothing they made is
deleted.

**Activity** lists who changed what in the organization.

## The organization wallet

The organization wallet signs the organization's plans and pays its Dev
tier. The Owner sets it in **Organization settings**, on the **Organization
wallet** card:

1. Connect the wallet.
2. Sign a message. It costs nothing and moves nothing.
3. Enter the code Mesub emails you.

In a personal organization, it is the wallet your Mesub account signs with,
until you register another one there.

The organization wallet is not the **receiving wallet**: each plan names the
wallets it pays into, up to four. The Owner and Admins can change a plan's
receiving wallet, through To sign.

## To sign

**To sign** lists the plans and actions waiting for a signature: a new plan,
ending, closing or renaming a plan, changing a plan's receiving wallet,
starting or cancelling the Dev tier.

- **Who signs**: whoever holds the wallet's key, whatever their role.
- **Who refuses**: only the wallet that signs it.
- **Who cancels**: the person who prepared it, or the Owner or an Admin.

Nobody is emailed. To ask someone to sign, copy the link of a row and send
it.

## Sign and publish

The last button of **New plan** depends on the wallet you are signed in
with:

| Your situation                                    | The button                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| You hold the organization wallet                  | **Sign and publish**: the plan goes on chain now    |
| Someone else holds it                             | **Send to To sign**: the plan waits for that wallet |
| No organization wallet yet, you are the Owner     | **Connect a wallet**                                |
| No organization wallet yet, you are not the Owner | None: the Owner sets the wallet first               |

A plan an agent prepares with `prepare_plan` waits in To sign the same way.

## Tiers and Billing

A tier belongs to a project: see [pricing and limits](./pricing.mdx). It
changes in **Billing**, and only the Owner changes it.

- In a personal organization, **Upgrade to Dev** is paid by your Mesub
  account's wallet.
- In any other organization, **Start Dev** and **Cancel Dev** wait in To
  sign, and the organization wallet pays.
