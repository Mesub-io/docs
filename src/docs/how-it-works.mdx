Mesub is **recurring billing for Solana wallets**, built on the
[Solana Foundation's subscriptions program](https://github.com/solana-foundation/subscriptions).
Everything it does fits in three moments.

<Flow>
  <Moment step={1} tone='orange' who='Your customer' title='Approves a plan, once'>
    Two approvals in their wallet: the terms, then the first payment.
  </Moment>
  <Moment step={2} tone='violet' who='Mesub' title='Charges it every period'>
    The price goes from their wallet to yours. A missed charge is retried.
  </Moment>
  <Moment step={3} tone='teal' who='Your server' title='Asks who has access'>
    One question before serving anything paid. Mesub answers yes or no.
  </Moment>
</Flow>

## Who does what

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="You" icon="lucide:store">
    Publish a **plan** in the dashboard: a price, a token, a period, and the
    wallet that receives the money.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your customer" icon="lucide:wallet">
    Pays from their **own wallet**, any that speaks the
    [Wallet Standard](https://github.com/wallet-standard/wallet-standard). No
    Mesub account: they stay signed in to your site.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mesub" icon="lucide:repeat">
    Sends each charge when it is due, **retries** a missed one, and keeps the
    record of every attempt.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Built on the Solana Foundation's program

The money never passes through Mesub. Every plan and every subscription lives
in the **[subscriptions program](https://github.com/solana-foundation/subscriptions)**
published by the Solana Foundation: open source under the
[MIT licence](https://github.com/solana-foundation/subscriptions/blob/main/LICENSE)
and [audited three times by Cantina](https://github.com/solana-foundation/subscriptions/blob/main/audits/AUDIT_STATUS.md).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="What the program does" icon="lucide:shield-check">
    Holds the plan's terms on chain and lets the price be pulled **once per
    period**, to the plan's destination, and nothing more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What Mesub adds" icon="lucide:layers">
    Sends each pull on time, retries the ones that fail, records every attempt,
    and turns the result into the **access** your server reads.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
Mesub is **non-custodial**. A subscription is a
[capped delegation](https://github.com/solana-foundation/subscriptions#overview)
the customer can cancel on chain at any time, with or without Mesub.
</Note>

## What you build

<Steps>
<Step title="A plan">

Created in the dashboard, **no code**. Its price, token and period are signed
on chain and cannot change after.

</Step>
<Step title="A check on your server">

Install **[`@mesub/node`](https://github.com/Mesub-io/node-sdk)**, give it your API key, and ask `hasAccess` before
serving anything paid. This is the only code Mesub requires.

</Step>
<Step title="A way to subscribe">

The ready-made button of **[`@mesub/react`](https://github.com/Mesub-io/react-sdk)**, or your own button calling your
server. Either way, your pages never hold a key and never talk to Mesub.

</Step>
</Steps>
