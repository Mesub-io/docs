Mesub is **recurring billing for Solana wallets**. A customer approves a plan
once; from then on Mesub pulls the plan's price from their wallet to yours at
every period, and your server asks Mesub who has access.

## The three parties

**You, the merchant**, publish a plan from the dashboard: a price, a token, a
period, and the wallet that receives the money. Those terms are **signed on
chain** when the plan is created and cannot change after.

**Your customer** pays from their own wallet. They have **no Mesub account**:
they stay signed in to your site, with your login. Subscribing takes two
approvals in their wallet, the terms and then the first payment.

**Mesub** sends each charge when it is due, retries a missed one, keeps the
record of every attempt, and answers your server.

<Aside>
<Note>
Mesub is **non-custodial**. The wallet delegates the right to pull the plan's
price, once per period, to the plan's destination. Nothing else can be taken.
</Note>
</Aside>

## From a click to a renewal

```diagram
  your pages                  your server (@mesub/node)                  Mesub
 ────────────                ───────────────────────────                ───────

 "Subscribe"        ───────>  subscriptions.create          ───────>  reserves it, builds
                                                                      the terms and the
 wallet signs       <───────  terms + unsigned transaction  <───────  transaction
 terms, then
 the payment        ───────>  subscriptions.submit          ───────>  co-signs, sends,
                                                                      first period paid

 a paid page        ───────>  hasAccess(customer, 'pro')    ───────>  yes or no, cached

                              your webhook endpoint         <───────  renewed, late,
                                                                      stopped, ended
```

Your pages never talk to Mesub and never hold a key. **Everything goes through
your server**, which is the only place the API key lives.

## What you build

1. **A plan**, in the dashboard. No code.
2. **A check on your server.** Install `@mesub/node`, give it your API key, and
   ask `hasAccess` before serving anything paid. This is the only required
   code.
3. **A way to subscribe.** Either your own button calling your server, or the
   ready-made window of `@mesub/react`, which is optional and calls the routes
   `@mesub/node` mounts for it.

## What Mesub knows about a customer

Mesub finds a customer by **your own id for them** (`external_id`), by the
**wallet that pays**, or by the **email** given when they subscribed. Pass your
id when your app has a login: access then follows the person, whichever wallet
they pay from.
